# POTA rules
- POTA has NO antenna rule: no height, tree, stake or
  footprint limit. Everything is delegated to the park.
- "The conditions of your radio license, all local and
  federal laws, and regulations must be followed; they
  supersede anything contained in these documents"
- Station and operator inside the park boundary, on
  public property; trails/rivers: within 100 ft (30.5 m)
- Vehicles parked on public property in the park: OK
- No trespass across private land to reach the park
## The park decides
- Rules differ between neighbouring parks of one agency
- NPS: 36 CFR + each park's Superintendent's Compendium
- Stakes are governed separately from tree attachment
- Throwing a line can itself be the violation
  (36 CFR 2.1(a)(3): no tossing items down hillsides)
- Ambiguous? Ask the land manager before you deploy
---
## POTA guidance (advisory)
- "Many parks prohibit wires or ropes from being put
  into trees or stakes in the ground."
- Do not use trees on a campsite unless staff approve
- Flag guy lines, stakes and feedlines with high-vis
  tape or small cones
- Leave No Trace: pick up feedlines and radials
- Large stations, generators, towers: coordinate with
  park staff; permits take time; "NO" is an answer
- Historic or cultural sites may not suit a station

Source: docs.pota.app rules, footprint and activator
guides; 36 CFR 1.5, 2.1 (portvert siting-rules)
