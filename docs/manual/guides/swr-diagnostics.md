# SWR diagnostics
## SWR high everywhere (>5)
- Open or short in the feedline: MEASURE -> CABLE (S11)
- Connector not tight; adapter missing from the cal
- Calibration off: status shows no C letter
## Dip in the wrong place
- Element length: 468/f ft (dipole), 234/f (1/4 wave)
- Nearby metal, height above ground shift f0 down
- Coax on a vertical acts as radial: add a choke
---
## Dip is broad and shallow
- Lossy coax or wet connectors: SWR looks good, is not
- Compare SWR vs SWR ANT: big gap = big feedline loss
- Poor ground/radials on a vertical: R too high
## Reading jumps around
- Loose connector, moving cable during the sweep
- Nearby transmitter: pause the sweep, retry
- Battery low: charge; noisy readings first at 900 MHz
