# Wire or vertical?
## 40 m
- Trees and attaching allowed -> WIRE. Signals arrive
  at 45-90 degrees, where a low wire is strongest.
- Trees but attaching prohibited -> mast with a sloper
  or inverted V, or a vertical
## 20 m and up
- Nothing arrives above about 45 degrees -> VERTICAL
- No trees (prairie, beach, alpine, car park) ->
  the vertical is the right answer, not a compromise
## Support
- With a vehicle: drive-on or hitch mount; bond it
  into the ground system deliberately
- On foot: telescoping fibreglass mast or a short riser;
  now the weight budget bites
