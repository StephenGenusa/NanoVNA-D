# Ferrite mixes
| Mix | Class | ui | Suppression | Use |
|---|---|--:|---|---|
| 31 | MnZn | 1500 | 1-500 MHz | HF chokes 160-20 m |
| 43 | NiZn | 850 | 20-250 MHz | chokes 40-10 m, 6/2 m |
| 44 | NiZn | 500 | 30-500 MHz | high-resistivity 43 |
| 52 | NiZn | 250 | 200-1000 MHz | W2FMI transformers |
| 61 | NiZn | 125 | above 200 MHz | VHF/UHF; baluns |
| 73 | MnZn | 2500 | below 30 MHz | small beads only |
| 75 | MnZn | 5000 | AM BC, LF | pulse transformers |

- ui = initial permeability. MnZn mixes are high-mu and
  lossy low in frequency; NiZn work from a few MHz up.
- #31 exhibits both resonances of the choke model, so one
  #31 choke covers about an octave more than #43 and
  holds up better with temperature on the low HF bands
- Lossy is a feature in a choke: you want R, not X
- Verify a bead or core with the analyser: unmarked
  cores are common and mixes are easily confused

Source: Fair-Rite catalog 14th ed. and 31 data sheet;
Palomar mix table (52); K9YC RFI, Ferrites and Chokes
