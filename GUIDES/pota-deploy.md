# Field deploy
## Sequence: the order is the trick
1. Pick the spot. Check what THIS park allows.
2. GROUND SYSTEM FIRST, before anything else.
3. Riser up. Guy it. One guy set beats a stiffer mast.
4. Whip, coil, feedpoint. Check the thread pitch
   (3/8-24 vs M10x1.0 vs M10x1.5 cross-thread).
5. Choke at the feedpoint. Coax away perpendicular
   to the radials, not along them.
6. NOW tune. Cut long, fold back.
Pack-down is the list backwards. Count your stakes.
## Analyser steps
- Calibrate at the end of the jumper (cal-checklist)
- SWR trace, marker SEARCH MINIMUM, RESONANCE panel
- Save the sweep to the card per iteration

## Pack check
- Radials on separate winders, insulators for the tips
- Screen or cloth, stakes, bonding wire
- Riser, guys, tensioners, mount, spare hardware
- Whip, coil, thread adapters
- Coax (shortest that reaches), feedpoint choke
- Analyser, tape measure, pen and log
## When the site fights you
- No anchors: weight the tips; keep the pair opposed
- Solid rock: elevate rather than fight it
- Wet ground: better ground; don't stake into water
- Wind: guy first, tune second
- Too small for 1/4 wave radials: shorter and more
- One direction only: two radials in line, not one
- SWR wild: stop tuning, fit the choke
