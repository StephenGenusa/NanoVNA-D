# Radials
## Elevate them
- 4 elevated radials are within 0.2 dB of 64 on the
  ground. Four on the ground: about -5 dB.
- 1 radial: +0.77 dB toward it, -3.34 dB off the back
- Only two? Put them OPPOSITE, not at 90 degrees
- Tip height decouples you: single radial -> 0.1 wl
- No wire from the junction to a ground stake: measured
  0.5 dB worse and more current asymmetry
- Wire #20-#24 stranded; gauge barely matters, count does
## Precut lengths, ft (about 234/f up to 20 m)
| Band | 40 m | 30 m | 20 m | 17 m | 15 m | 10 m |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| ft | 33 | 23.5 | 16.5 | 12.5 | 10.2 | 6.4 |

17 m and up are shorter than 234/f (10 m only 78%).
40 m radials are a half wave on 20 m: carry a 20 m set.
Tune with the whip, not the radials.
