# SOTA rules
- Activation zone: within 25 vertical metres of the
  summit (the contour 25 m below the peak)
- All equipment carried to the site by the activation
  team
- Operate from a portable power source: batteries,
  solar; no fossil-fuel generators
- Not in or near a motor vehicle; no part of the
  station connected to a vehicle
- Points need at least 4 QSOs, each with a different
  station (chasers may work you from anywhere)
- Land access rules still apply: SOTA grants no
  permission; check the summit's access notes
## Practical
- Masts: fibreglass telescoping; guy first, then tune
- Weight budget: radials on winders, one choke, short
  coax; a short whip on 20 m beats a heavy wire kit
- Wind moves a whip and its resonance: guy it

Source: SOTA General Rules (sota.org.uk)
